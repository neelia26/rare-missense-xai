# Conservation selection decision

## BEST OBSERVED CONDITION

All three conservation measures produced the highest observed training CV mean MCC (0.72214).

## PARSIMONIOUS ALTERNATIVE

phyloP + PhastCons produced mean MCC 0.72082 using one fewer conservation score.

## STATISTICALLY UNCERTAIN DIFFERENCE

The paired gene-cluster bootstrap MCC difference (all three minus phyloP + PhastCons) was 0.00132, with 95% interval [-0.00077, 0.00341] and P(difference > 0)=0.888. The interval includes zero. Therefore, the larger configuration is not claimed superior; phyloP + PhastCons is the parsimonious alternative for later confirmation.

This is a post-run refinement. It does not replace the preserved initial recommendation and uses no validation or test outcomes.
